### Added

- **A Pregnancy tab.** While a pregnancy is declared, a Pregnancy tab appears beside Calendar and
  Insights (and in the mobile tab bar). It shows the same week, trimester and countdown as the
  dashboard card, the owner's journal totals since the pregnancy began (days logged and average
  mood), and a week-by-week timeline from week 4 to the due-date week — or to the current week when
  the pregnancy runs past it — with each week's size comparison, development note, usual checks and
  the days logged and average mood for that week. The tab and `/pregnancy` exist only while a
  pregnancy is active; without one the address redirects to the dashboard. No migration.

### Fixed

- The pregnancy countdown on the dashboard read "days until the due date" with no number. It now
  reads, for example, "244 days until the due date" in every language.
